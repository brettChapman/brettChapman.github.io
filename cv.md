## Professional profile

Bioinformatician and data scientist with over 15 years' experience leading large genomics and multi-omics research projects across academia and the biotechnology industry, including next-generation and long-read sequencing data. Extensive experience in statistical modelling and high-performance computing (HPC), with broad skills in genome assembly, pangenomics, transcriptomics, proteogenomics, proteomics, metabolomics and structural variation analysis.

I contribute to international genome consortia for wheat, barley and oat, with publications in *Nature*, *Nature Genetics* and *Science*. I build reusable analytical workflows and bioinformatics pipelines, develop software in Python and R, manage HPC infrastructure, integrate multi-omics data, and work with diverse research teams to generate biological insight.

### Research highlights

- Led construction of the first barley pangenome graph, spanning 20 and later 76 genomes (first author, *Agriculture Communications* 2026).
- Contributed to international pangenome studies of barley (*Nature* 2024), the barley pan-transcriptome (*Nature Genetics* 2025), the Hordeum I genome (*Nature Plants* 2025) and hexaploid oat (*Nature* 2026).
- Co-author on the IWGSC bread wheat genome (*Science* 2014) and the IBSC barley reference genome (*Nature* 2017).
- Developed a bioinformatics framework for proteogenomics (PhD), presented at PAG Asia and the Australia–China Centre for Wheat Improvement in Beijing (2013).
- Designed and developed MultiVis, an open-source statistical analysis and visualisation framework for multi-omics data.
- Longitudinal RNA-seq with repeated-measures mixed-effects models, and variant analysis with functional consequence assessment, in clinical cohorts for rare-disease RNA therapeutic programs.

## Research and technical expertise

**Genomics and pangenomics.** Pangenome graph construction and analysis (PGGB, Minigraph-Cactus, vg, odgi); genome assembly, annotation and gene prediction; structural variation and CNV analysis; population genomics; GWAS and linkage disequilibrium; genotyping from Illumina and PacBio HiFi data; comparative genomics; variant annotation (VEP).

**Transcriptomics.** Pan-transcriptome analysis; bulk RNA-seq (STAR, Salmon); longitudinal differential expression (limma, edgeR, DESeq2) with ORA and GSEA; single-cell RNA-seq (Seurat, Scanpy, Monocle3) and TCR repertoire analysis (scirpy, scRepertoire).

**Proteomics and multi-omics.** Proteogenomics; tandem mass spectrometry; qualitative and quantitative metabolomics; integrative multi-omics (DIABLO/mixOmics); genotype–phenotype correlation.

**Statistics and machine learning.** Linear and mixed-effects models, ANOVA, regression, PCA, PLS/PLS-DA, MDS, t-SNE, UMAP; statistical analysis planning; supervised and unsupervised learning in scikit-learn with cross-validation, ROC-AUC and SHAP; Azure Machine Learning and MLflow (working knowledge).

**Software and computing.** Python, R, SQL, Bash, MATLAB, JavaScript (D3.js), Streamlit; published PyPI packages; Git/GitHub; Nextflow; Docker, Singularity, Conda, pixi, uv, Poetry; HPC (Slurm); AWS, GCP, Azure; AI-assisted development.

**Visualisation and delivery.** Dataiku, Tableau, Power BI, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, interactive dashboards, Jupyter and Quarto reporting; Agile delivery (Asana), Miro.
