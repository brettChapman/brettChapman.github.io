Bioinformatics consulting

Short, well-scoped analysis projects for research groups and biotech companies in precision medicine and precision agriculture. Scoped to your question and your budget.

Start a conversation See past work

How I can help

RNA-seq & transcriptomics

QC, alignment and quantification, differential expression including longitudinal and repeated-measures designs, pathway enrichment (ORA, GSEA) and clear figures.

Genomics & variants

Variant annotation and interpretation, structural variation, GWAS, genome assembly and annotation, and pangenome graph construction.

Multi-omics & statistics

Proteomics, transcriptomics and metabolomics analysis, multivariate statistics, integrative analysis and statistical analysis.

Pipelines & HPC

Reproducible workflows with Nextflow, Docker and Singularity, Slurm on HPC or cloud, and help preparing Pawsey compute allocation applications.

Reports & figures

Clear Jupyter and Quarto reports and publication-ready figures that explain results to non-technical colleagues and reviewers.

Training & mentoring

Hands-on sessions in Python, R, statistics and bioinformatics for lab members, students and early-career researchers.

Who I work with

  • University and institute research groups without a dedicated bioinformatician
  • Biotech start-ups that need analysis capacity before hiring
  • Precision medicine and RNA therapeutics teams working with sequencing and clinical cohort data
  • Plant breeding and precision agriculture groups working with crop genomes

How it works

  1. Intro call. A short call to understand your question, data and timeline.
  2. Scope and quote. A written scope with deliverables and a fixed quote or block of hours that fits your budget.
  3. Analysis. Regular check-ins so you see results early and can steer.
  4. Handover. A report, figures and documented, reproducible code you own.

Practical details

  • Based in Perth (AWST, UTC+8), working remotely with clients across Australia and the Asia-Pacific.
  • Australian ABN-registered sole trader, invoicing in AUD.
  • Happy to work under an NDA, and within your own computing environment when data can't leave it.
