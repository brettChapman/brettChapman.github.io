About me

I'm a bioinformatician and data scientist based in Perth, Western Australia, with more than 15 years leading genomics and multi-omics projects across universities and the biotech industry. I turn large, complex biological datasets (short- and long-read sequencing, genomics, RNA-seq, proteomics and metabolomics) into clear, reproducible results that research and clinical teams can act on.

Most recently I was Senior Scientist in Bioinformatics at PYC Therapeutics, analysing longitudinal RNA-seq and clinical variant data for rare-disease therapeutic programs. Before that I spent four years as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Western Crop Genetics Alliance, Murdoch University, where I built the first barley pangenome graph and contributed to international pangenome consortia for barley and oat.

My work has appeared in Nature, Nature Genetics, Nature Plants and Science, including the IWGSC bread wheat genome and the IBSC barley reference genome. I write analysis software in Python and R, run workloads on HPC and cloud, and enjoy explaining complex results to people who don't live in a terminal.

What I do

Genomics & pangenomics

Genome assembly and annotation, pangenome graphs (PGGB, minigraph, vg, odgi), structural variation, GWAS, genotyping and variant annotation.

Transcriptomics

Bulk RNA-seq (STAR, Salmon), longitudinal differential expression with mixed-effects models (limma, edgeR, DESeq2), ORA/GSEA, and single-cell RNA-seq.

Multi-omics & statistics

Proteogenomics, proteomics and metabolomics, multivariate statistics (PCA, PLS-DA, ANOVA, mixed-effects models), integrative analysis, statistical analysis and machine learning in scikit-learn.

Pipelines, HPC & reporting

Reproducible workflows (Nextflow, Docker, Singularity, Slurm), Pawsey and cloud computing, and Dataiku, Jupyter and Quarto reporting.
